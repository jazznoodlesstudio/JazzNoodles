# About Me

## Hello, I'm the mind behind Jazznoodles.

**Connecting ideas. Creating possibilities.**

I’m someone who enjoys exploring how things work, connecting ideas across different disciplines, and turning curiosity into something meaningful.

My professional journey started in semiconductor engineering, then moved through system analysis and project management, eventually leading me into product management in the cloud technology industry.

Along the way, I discovered that what fascinates me most is not just technology itself, but how technology, people, systems, and business come together to create something valuable.

I enjoy looking at the bigger picture, understanding how different pieces connect, and finding ways to turn complex challenges into practical solutions.

## What I Explore

My curiosity doesn't stay in one place.

- **Technology** — Cloud computing, AI, system architecture, and emerging technologies.
    
- **Product & Business** — Product strategy, platform ecosystems, SaaS, and turning ideas into viable products.
    
- **Faith & Reflection** — Exploring biblical principles, personal growth, and the relationship between faith and everyday life.
    
- **Creative Studio** — Art, visual experiments, storytelling, and creative expressions.
    
- **Travel & Discovery** — Exploring new places, experiencing different cultures, and collecting stories along the way.
    

## How I Think

I believe interesting ideas often happen at the intersection of different fields.

An engineering mindset helps me understand how things work. Product thinking helps me understand why they matter. Creativity encourages me to imagine what else is possible.

I'm drawn to questions, not just answers — especially questions that challenge assumptions, reveal new perspectives, or connect seemingly unrelated ideas.

I don't expect to have everything figured out. I believe learning is an ongoing process of exploring, experimenting, making mistakes, and discovering better ways forward.

## Why Jazznoodles?

Jazz represents creativity, improvisation, and the freedom to explore.

Noodles represent different ingredients coming together — each with its own character, yet creating something new when combined.

That's how I see ideas, too.

Technology, business, faith, art, and life may seem like different worlds, but I enjoy finding the connections between them.

**Jazznoodles is my space to explore, connect, create, and share what I discover along the way.**

Welcome to my little corner of the internet.

_Explore. Connect. Create._
